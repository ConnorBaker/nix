The `flush()`-then-trailer pattern is uniform; the Brotli pair specifically could share a `ChunkedCompressionSink::finish()` that calls a virtual `drainTrailer()`.
    - ../verified/01-libutil-io.md
    - **Validation:** VALID — but marginal. The other three sinks don't subclass `ChunkedCompressionSink` and won't share. Honest "small win" rather than a debt entry; keep but accept the marginality. Effort: trivial.

A `Renderable<T>` concept (`render(store)` / `parse(store, sv)`) plus a generic store-prefix wrapper would consolidate the `<storeDir>/<base>(<suffix>)` formatting.
